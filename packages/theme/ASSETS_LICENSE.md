# Asset licensing

The MIT License in `LICENSE` covers the project's source code and documentation. Files under `assets/` are licensed per entry in `assets/manifest.yaml` and are not covered by MIT merely because they appear in this repository.

Every distributable asset must record its creator, source, generation method, human edits, license, and attribution requirements before it is included in a release. An absent manifest entry grants no reuse permission beyond rights required to view the repository.

Project-owned original or AI-assisted assets should normally use `CC-BY-4.0` or `CC0-1.0`, chosen deliberately per asset. Third-party references, franchise characters, logos, living-artist imitation, unlicensed fonts, and materials without redistribution rights are not accepted.
